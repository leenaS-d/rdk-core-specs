Core RDK Video (RDK-V) defines the common platform foundation for RDK-based video solutions through governed capabilities, standardized interfaces, lifecycle policies, and conformance requirements. It enables ecosystem-wide interoperability and portability while preserving flexibility for innovation and differentiation.

RDK8 advances application infrastructure through Firebolt Connect, enabling standardized and scalable delivery of video services, web applications, and native applications across RDK-powered devices.

## RDK8 Release Overview

Released as a Development Preview, RDK8 enables early validation of key capabilities, accelerates ecosystem readiness, and reduces integration risk. It also provides a clear signal of platform direction, helping operators and partners plan product roadmaps and investments with greater confidence.

## Explore the Core RDK platform

### Components Catalog

Explore the RDK8 Components Catalog, connecting application-facing capabilities with middleware and vendor-layer implementations.

### Northbound APIs

Standardized APIs the middleware exposes upward to the application layer, giving apps consistent access to device capabilities via Thunder and Firebolt.

### Southbound APIs

The Hardware Abstraction Layer (HAL) between middleware and the platform layer — standardized interfaces that abstract hardware differences.

## Key benefits of RDK8

### Early validation of next-generation app delivery

Gives developers and partners an opportunity to validate standardized app onboarding and integration through Firebolt Connect, including support for new App Managers and container-based technologies such as DAC 2.0, while reducing late-stage risk.

### Accelerated app lifecycle readiness

Enables earlier validation of app lifecycle behavior through Firebolt Connect, helping ensure alignment with W3C standards, Firebolt 8.0, and more consistent app behavior across devices.

### Faster ecosystem enablement and feedback

Allows developers, OEMs, and silicon partners to test on real implementations sooner, gather practical feedback, and reduce fragmentation by enabling vendors in parallel rather than sequentially.

### Lower risk for operators

Helps operators assess new capabilities early without impacting live deployments, supporting more confident planning and adoption.

### Faster app time to market at scale

Standardized app onboarding and validation through Firebolt Connect streamline optimization and support repeatable deployment across the RDK ecosystem.

### Early performance and stability insights

Gives developers visibility into CPU, memory, GPU, and I/O performance on real hardware before field deployment.

### Lower long-term platform costs and smoother user experience

Improved performance and power efficiency, driven in part by Vulkan adoption, help reduce long-term costs while enhancing the user experience.

### Revamped OSS Model

Enables flexible, platform-specific OSS integration, improving adaptability and driving greater community reuse and contributions.
